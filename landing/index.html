How it works

Three steps to launch

From zero to organized in under a minute.

Add a project

Pick any folder on your Mac. Projekt handles the rest.

Set up commands

Configure launch actions and custom shell commands per project.

Launch and go

One click opens Finder, your editor, and terminal. Done.

Your data stays on your machine.

Projekt stores everything in a local SQLite database. No account required, no cloud sync, no telemetry. Just your projects, right where they belong.
